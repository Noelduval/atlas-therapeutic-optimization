# Scientific Decisions

## SD-011 — Why Atlas v1 Uses the VITA Aβ Metalloprotease System Rather Than Antibody Benchmarks

### Decision

Use the DP622-S2 / Aβ42 / S2 VITA system as Atlas v1’s sole flagship benchmark.

### Context

This system exercises constrained protein candidate generation, enzyme–substrate
structural reasoning, zinc catalytic geometry, substrate recognition, selectivity
risk, model disagreement, scientific critique, and blinded retrospective
comparison in one Alzheimer’s-relevant program.

### Alternative rejected

HER2/trastuzumab and other antibody-specific therapeutic benchmark systems.

The alternative is scientifically valid but would move v1 away from its locked
Alzheimer’s-centered motivation and from the catalytic and enzyme–substrate
constraints that define this challenge. It is therefore absent from adapters,
datasets, evaluators, UI, demos, fixtures, and campaign configurations.

## PHGDH mechanism case study — future research

PHGDH is a **future mechanism/modality case study only**. It may inform later
research about transcriptional regulation, small-molecule optimization,
blood-brain-barrier pharmacology, causal disease mechanisms, and therapeutic
modality selection. It has no v1 adapter, evaluator, model, dataset, campaign, UI
path, fixture, or test requirement.
